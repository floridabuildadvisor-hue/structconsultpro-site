Concrete takeoffs in CY, by element and sheet

Cubic yards for footings, slabs, grade beams, columns, tie beams and other cast-in-place elements as drawn, with slab and forming areas alongside, all traced to the foundation and structural sheets. For Florida contractors bidding residential and light commercial concrete.

What gets counted

Quantities, units and the sheets they come from.

Footings
CY and LF by mark from the footing schedule, including grade beams. From the foundation plan.
Slabs
SF and CY by thickness and area. Thickened edges and slab steps are listed separately.
Tie beams & columns
CY and LF by mark, as drawn. Common on Florida concrete block (CBS) homes, where they are listed per level.
Forming
SFCA (square feet of contact area) by element, when you ask for it in the scope.
Rebar
Only when it is in your scope and scheduled on the drawings: by bar size and length. Otherwise excluded and listed.
Under-slab
SF of vapor retarder where drawn.

How it's measured

Counted from the documents. Traced to the sheet. Questions, not guesses.

  1. 01 Net volume

    Volumes are net as drawn, with no waste or overpour added. You set your own factor.

  2. 02 By element mark

    Each footing, beam and column mark from the schedules gets its own row, so a revision to F2 changes one line.

  3. 03 Sheet-traced

    Every quantity points to the foundation or structural sheet it came from, with the revision.

What stays with you

Your prices. Your estimate.

Concrete and pump pricing, waste and overpour, labor and markup stay with you. We don't design reinforcement or check structural adequacy: we quantify what the structural drawings show.

Scope: reading and quantifying what is drawn, item counts, measurements, and organized lists. Not engineering judgment, not structural calculation, not code compliance review, and not a substitute for a licensed engineer's seal where one is required.
