Drywall takeoffs from your plan set

Square feet of board by type and thickness, linear feet of trims and bead, and openings counted, organized by area and traced to the sheet they came from. For Florida contractors and drywall subs bidding residential and light commercial work.

What gets counted

Quantities, units and the sheets they come from.

Board
SF by type and thickness (for example 1/2 in. regular, 5/8 in. Type X, moisture-resistant, cement board where drawn), split by wall type and area. From the floor plans, wall-type legend and sections.
Ceilings
SF by ceiling type and height from the reflected ceiling plan. Soffits and bulkheads are listed as their own rows.
Trims
LF of corner bead, J-bead or L-bead and control joints where the drawings show them.
Openings
EA by size, deducted from wall area. Areas are net as drawn unless you state another rule.
Heights
Taken from sections and interior elevations. A height that isn't dimensioned becomes a question.

How it's measured

Counted from the documents. Traced to the sheet. Questions, not guesses.

  1. 01 Net as drawn

    Areas are measured net of openings with no waste added. Your waste factor goes in your estimate, where you control it.

  2. 02 By wall type

    Each wall type in the legend gets its own rows, so a 5/8 in. Type X separation wall never blends into the 1/2 in. partitions.

  3. 03 Faces as detailed

    One face or two follows the wall-type detail. Where the detail doesn't say, it goes on the questions list.

What stays with you

Your prices. Your estimate.

Board prices, sheet layout and waste, labor and markup stay with you. We don't select fire-rated assemblies or decide where Type X board is required: we count what the wall-type legend and plans show.

Scope: reading and quantifying what is drawn, item counts, measurements, and organized lists. Not engineering judgment, not structural calculation, not code compliance review, and not a substitute for a licensed engineer's seal where one is required.
