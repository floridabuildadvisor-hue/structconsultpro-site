Quantity takeoff · Doors & windows

Door and window takeoffs, plan checked against schedule

Every door and window counted by mark, size and type, with the count on the plans checked against the door and window schedules. Mismatches come back as questions before you price a single unit.

What gets counted

Quantities, units and the sheets they come from.

Doors
EA by mark, size and type, interior and exterior separate. From the floor plans and door schedule.
Windows
EA by mark, size and type, with rough opening sizes where scheduled. From the floor plans, elevations and window schedule.
Hardware
EA by hardware set, only where a hardware schedule exists.
Approval numbers
When the schedule lists a Florida Product Approval or Miami-Dade NOA number, it is copied into the row as written.
Shutters
EA or SF of opening protection, where it is drawn or scheduled as its own item.
Trim
LF of casing and frames, when they are in your scope.

How it's measured

Counted from the documents. Traced to the sheet. Questions, not guesses.

  1. 01 Plan against schedule

    Each mark is counted on the plans and compared with the schedule quantity. Both numbers are shown if they differ.

  2. 02 One row per mark

    Size and type come from the schedule, so supplier quotes line up with your takeoff.

  3. 03 Approvals copied as written

    Product approval or NOA numbers are carried over so your supplier quote can reference them. We don't verify approvals or decide which product is required.

What stays with you

Your prices. Your estimate.

Unit prices, supplier selection, installation and markup stay with you. We don't check impact ratings, design pressures or product approvals: whether a product suits an opening is for the design professional and the building department.

Scope: reading and quantifying what is drawn, item counts, measurements, and organized lists. Not engineering judgment, not structural calculation, not code compliance review, and not a substitute for a licensed engineer's seal where one is required.
