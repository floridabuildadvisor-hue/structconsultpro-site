Quantity takeoff · Framing

Framing takeoffs, counted from the sheets

Linear feet of wall by wall type, studs at the spacing drawn, plates, headers from the header schedule and sheathing area, organized by level and traced to the sheet. For wood and light-gauge framing on Florida residential and light commercial projects.

What gets counted

Quantities, units and the sheets they come from.

Walls
LF by wall type and height, exterior and interior separate. From the floor plans and wall-type legend.
Studs
EA at the spacing shown on the plans or wall-type detail. Extra studs at corners and openings follow a rule you state in the scope; otherwise they are listed as excluded.
Plates
LF of bottom and top plates, single or double as the wall-type detail shows.
Headers
EA by size and length from the header schedule or as called out on plan.
Sheathing
SF of wall and roof sheathing where drawn, by type, openings deducted.
Connectors
EA only where the plans or schedules call them out, copied as written.

How it's measured

Counted from the documents. Traced to the sheet. Questions, not guesses.

  1. 01 Spacing as drawn

    Stud counts use the spacing on the plans. Where none is shown, that's a question, not an assumption.

  2. 02 Members as specified

    Sizes, grades and headers are copied from the drawings and schedules. We don't size, substitute or design members.

  3. 03 Level by level

    Quantities are split by level and wall type, so you can stage material and compare sub bids line by line.

What stays with you

Your prices. Your estimate.

Lumber prices, cut lists and waste, labor and markup stay with you. We don't size members, check spans or decide connector requirements: we count what the plans and schedules show.

Scope: reading and quantifying what is drawn, item counts, measurements, and organized lists. Not engineering judgment, not structural calculation, not code compliance review, and not a substitute for a licensed engineer's seal where one is required.
