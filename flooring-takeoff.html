What gets counted

Quantities, units and the sheets they come from.

Floor finishes
SF by finish code (tile, luxury vinyl, carpet, wood, sealed concrete) and room. From the finish plan and finish schedule.
Base
LF by base type, openings deducted.
Wall tile
SF by finish code and height as drawn on the interior elevations.
Transitions
EA or LF where the finish changes at a doorway, when the plans show it.
Paint
SF of wall and ceiling by paint code, where the finish schedule calls it out.

How it's measured

Counted from the documents. Traced to the sheet. Questions, not guesses.

  1. 01 Room by room

    Each room on the finish schedule gets its own rows, so you can price by area or bundle rooms as you like.

  2. 02 Schedule against plan

    The finish schedule is checked against the finish plan. Where they disagree, it's a question.

  3. 03 Net, no pattern waste

    Areas are net as drawn. Pattern layout, diagonal waste and overage stay with you.

What stays with you

Your prices. Your estimate.

Material prices, pattern waste and overage, setting materials, labor and markup stay with you. Finish selections come from the documents: we don't choose or substitute products.

Scope: reading and quantifying what is drawn, item counts, measurements, and organized lists. Not engineering judgment, not structural calculation, not code compliance review, and not a substitute for a licensed engineer's seal where one is required.
